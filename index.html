1.0The fit

A small firm rarely has one big problem: it has too many routine NDAs waiting on one paralegal, too many mornings that start with answering email instead of billable work, and too many engagement letters drafted from scratch every time.

Built for firms that want work handled. Your lawyers never learn a tool.

This is for you if

  • You run a small or boutique firm: contracts to review, letters to draft, client intake to answer.
  • Partners or staff are the bottleneck on repetitive document drafting and follow-ups.
  • You want it working wherever your team already works: email, Word, Teams, Slack. An attorney approves everything that leaves the firm.

This is not for

  • Firms looking for a chatbot to play with.
  • Firms that cannot give me one point of contact. I need a single person at your firm to work with; everything else is handled.
  • Firms that want the AI making the legal calls on its own. Everything she produces is reviewed and approved by your attorneys, by design.
